VR Escape Room

Level Designer & VR Developer · CS 417 – Virtual Reality · Spring 2026
Level DesignUnityVR Interaction
Play in VR Source Code

The Challenge

Build a physically-interactive VR escape room as a two-person team, where each partner owns an entire themed room end-to-end — without either room's puzzle logic or physical interactions feeling disconnected from the other's.

Process

My partner, Shanmukh Upadhyayula, designed a Medieval Fantasy themed room. I designed and built the connecting Ancient Greece themed room from scratch, so that players exploring a richly detailed Grecian environment could solve puzzles embedded in the space itself and escape.

Design Decisions

I was solely responsible for the Ancient Greece room. That meant Level Design & Art — designing and building the entire environment from scratch, including architecture, props, lighting, and atmosphere — alongside Puzzle Mechanics: authoring every interactive puzzle within the room and the logic that governs how each one triggers, progresses, and resolves.

On the technical side, I implemented the VR-native interactions players use to actually solve those puzzles — grabbing and examining objects with physical VR controls rather than menu-driven actions — and designed the UI/UX and win screen that confirms for the player the moment they've successfully completed all puzzles in the room.

Outcome

A working two-room Unity VR escape room with immersive level design, physical VR interactions, and a clear win condition — playable end-to-end.

What I'd Do Next

Playtest with people unfamiliar with the space to see where wayfinding or puzzle clarity breaks down, and explore cross-room puzzle dependencies that would make the two halves feel more like one connected experience rather than two adjacent rooms.
