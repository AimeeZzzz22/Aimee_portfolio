The Challenge

An open-ended VR game needs its core economy to feel meaningful — earning currency has to connect clearly to spending it, across two entirely different in-game environments, or the whole progression loop falls apart.

Process

Off The Clock is an open-ended VR game built by a team of four. Players earn money by grading papers in a classroom, then spend it at a weapon upgrade shop to power up before heading into combat. The game features a full resource economy, a persistent save system, tiered upgrade progression, and a poke-interactable shop UI.

I designed and engineered the complete store and economy system — the backbone of the game's progression loop — contributing 15 story points across 7 features.

Design Decisions

Resource Simulation (EconomyManager.cs): built the money system that tracks the player's currency and fires an OnMoneyChanged event whenever it updates. Money is earned by correctly grading papers in the classroom, creating a meaningful loop between the two environments.

Dual HUD Money Display: created two live money displays — one on the classroom monitor, one inside the mech cockpit — both driven by that same economy event, so the player always knows their balance no matter which environment they're in.

Weapon Upgrade Shop: designed the shop where players spend money to increase weapon damage, recalculating total damage from base damage plus all purchased boosts on every purchase, feeding directly into the combat system.

Tiered Unlock Progression: the shop has three tiers (Green → Orange → Blue) with two weapons per tier. Higher-tier cards stay hidden behind "???" with disabled buy buttons until the previous tier is purchased, so new interactables are literally revealed as players progress — with escalating costs shown on every card so players can plan their spending.

Inter-session Save System (SaveSystem.cs): a JSON-based save system that persists the player's money and each weapon's tier to disk, restoring all values before the first frame renders on reload.

Poke-Interactable Shop UI: implemented the shop's VR poke-interactable buttons — left arrow, right arrow, and buy — using XRSimpleInteractable and XRPokeFilter, each with a correctly configured box collider for front-facing poke detection.

Outcome

A complete, working resource economy — 15 story points across 7 features — that ties two separate game environments into one coherent progression loop, with persistent saves so progress isn't lost between sessions.

What I'd Bring to a Team

Owning the entire economy system taught me to think in systems, not features: every piece — the money event, the dual HUD, the upgrade tiers, the save file — had to stay in sync with every other piece, or the whole progression loop would break. That's the rigor I'd bring to a team: building the kind of interconnected system that keeps working as more features get layered on top of it, not just the one that demos well.
