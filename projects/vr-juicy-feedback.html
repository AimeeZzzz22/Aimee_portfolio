VR Game: Plants vs. Zombies

Gameplay & Interaction Designer · CS 417 – Virtual Reality · Spring 2026
UnityVR InteractionGame Feel
Playable Build Source Code

The Challenge

VR simulations can feel flat without strong sensory feedback — a "juicy" interactive experience needs the physical act of placing an object in the world to feel immediately rewarding, not just functionally correct. The assignment: build an interactive VR simulation as a team of four.

Process

By: Talha Coskun, Mireille Tan, Shanmukh Upadhyayula, and me. We built a VR simulation inspired by Plants vs. Zombies, where players fend off waves of zombies through a resource-driven loop — using sunshine to procure defensive plants and strategic enhancements. Core mechanics include Sunflower resource generation, Snowpea offensive combat, projectiles, and persistent buffs like a magnifying glass and watering can for economic and defensive efficiency.

Design Decisions

I built the Sunflower and Peashooter as self-contained Unity prefabs — their models, animations, and configurations — as the foundational building blocks of the resource and combat loop.

On interaction, I implemented the core VR interaction mechanics for placement: players physically pick up a plant card and place it onto the game grid, snapping into position and activating its behavior. That single interaction is what turns "spending a resource" into something you do with your hands rather than click through a menu.

I also focused on the "juice" itself — adding particle effects to plant actions, like pea projectile trails, so every placement and every shot produced visible, satisfying feedback instead of a silent state change.

Outcome

A playable VR defense simulation where resource management, physical plant placement, and reactive visual feedback combine into a real defensive progression loop against waves of zombies.

What I'd Do Next

Playtest the balance of resource costs against wave difficulty with real players, and extend the "juice" further — screen shake, haptic feedback — to more of the plant and combat actions.
